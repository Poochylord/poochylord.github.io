5. If your mod remains untouched for several weeks, you can nudge me in DMs for clarification.

Maps that are unrankable (rates maps, converts etc.);
AI generated / trend-based songs;

Very explicit songs (like borderline rule-breaking)

Songs with a 6* diff cap;

New mappers (but don't be afraid to submit regardless!);
LN, dump, density;
Songs with drain time in between 2:00 and 5:00 (longer songs are more often accepted if you ask me to mod a portion);
